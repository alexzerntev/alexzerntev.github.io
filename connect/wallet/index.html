Create your Wallet card

  1. Save the QR image to Photos on your iPhone.
  2. Open Wallet, tap +, then choose Create a Pass if available.
  3. Choose Create Pass Manually, select a template, and add your name, role, and QR code.
  4. In Edit Pass, use the palette control to choose a charcoal background and blue accent.
